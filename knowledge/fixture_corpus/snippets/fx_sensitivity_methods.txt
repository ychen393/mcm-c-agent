Method excerpt (structure only): after a baseline strategy is fixed, excellent papers probe
robustness by one-at-a-time and joint sweeps of transaction-cost parameters α_gold and
α_bitcoin, initial capital, and gold open-day calendars. They report how rank-order of
candidate policies changes when α doubles, and whether the recommended policy remains
above a buy-and-hold cash/gold/bitcoin benchmark. Sensitivity plots should use the same
causal evaluation window as the main results so that apparent robustness is not an artifact
of lookahead. When evidence is thin, the paper marks the claim as provisional rather than
citing nonexistent sources.
